PPG 008 Vanishing Point
"The unfolding of the desert is infinitely close to the timelessness of the film." "All techniques meant to unleash forces are techniques of disappearance." Baudrillard, Virilio.
PPG 007 Waves
The most open brief we've had. Waves: nautical, gravitational, quantum, interpersonal. Virginia Woolf, Eugenio Montale, Fernando Pessoa, Matsuo Bashō, Wisława Szymborska, Alice Oswald, Lucretius, and member work. "My yearnings burst into foam." "The useless trail of smoke behind a steamer."
"It can be the nautical kind. It can be gravity waves or wave-particle duality waves. It can be our interpersonal tidal forces."
PPG 006 Weird and Wayward Sisters
Rebecca Tamas, thesunthesunthesun. Hexes, spells, and the occult feminine in poetry. Women poets working at the edges of the permissible, in language, in form, in subject.
PPG 005 Modernism and Mysticism
AE's transcendent visions, Natural Magic, the Theosophists, The Quest Periodical. Dylan Thomas, Eva Gore-Booth. Surrealism, automatic writing, the unconscious as creative space. Rilke: "You must change your life."
PPG 004 The Margins of Modernism
Langston Hughes, "Paterson, the Strike," Mina Loy, Tender Buttons, almost-modernists.
PPG 003 The Winter Evening Settles Down
Desert places, vacant lots, who'd walk in this bleak place.
PPG 002 Détournement, Collage and Desire
"Take a newspaper. Take a pair of scissors..." Poetry as assemblage. Tristan Tzara, Dadaism, Détournement, the Cut-Up Method. Amy Gillies.
PPG 001 "The Image is More Than an Idea. It is a Vortex..."
Where it began. The poetic image as a charged, compressed unit of meaning. Ezra Pound, Imagism. Instant perception, condensation, the vortex.
"The apparition of these faces in the crowd;
Petals on a wet, black bough."

What is PPG

Peckham Poetry Group is a small, intimate poetry and creative reading group. Each session is a deep dive into a specific theme, movement, or poetic idea — often rooted in modernism, mysticism, fragmentation, or experimental form.

Sessions are built around carefully curated packs of primary texts — often original scans where possible. Participants encounter the poems directly. The atmosphere is intimate, candlelit, unhurried.

Format

Lights dimmed. LED candles. A microphone and amp passed between participants. A free write, followed by reading back. The printed pack is the object for the evening. No prior knowledge required.

Get involved

Sessions announced via mailing list and Instagram. To join, receive past packs, or submit writing — get in touch.

The full PDF packs are not hosted here, but they exist and you can have one.
